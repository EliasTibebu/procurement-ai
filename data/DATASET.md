# Procurement AI Training Dataset

## Objective

Train the model to understand procurement processes and behave
as an AI-native procurement assistant.

The model must understand procurement concepts but must not
invent regulations, thresholds, approvals, or legal requirements.

## Initial Skills

01. Procurement Need Classification

Classes:
- GOODS
- WORKS
- CONSULTANCY
- NON_CONSULTANCY

02. Missing Information Detection

Identify information required before a procurement need
can proceed.

03. Requirement Structuring

Transform unstructured procurement requests into structured
requirements.

04. Workspace Generation

Determine the appropriate procurement workspace and sections.

05. Specification Assistance

Assist with specifications without introducing unnecessary
brand restrictions.

06. TOR Assistance

Structure consultancy requirements into objectives, scope,
deliverables, experts, timeline and reporting requirements.

07. Procurement Risk Detection

Identify ambiguity, restrictive requirements, missing
information, contradictions and procurement risks.

08. Compliance Behavior

Never invent:
- procurement thresholds
- legal provisions
- approval authority
- mandatory timelines
- procurement methods based solely on assumptions

Retrieve authoritative rules before making such determinations.
